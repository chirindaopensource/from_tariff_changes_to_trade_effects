## Key callables, Tasks 1 to 27

This document discusses the 27 task orchestrators of the research pipeline which is presented in the `from_tariff_changes_to_trade_effects_draft.ipynb` notebook, one per task and in notebook order: `run_task_1` to `run_task_27`. 

Each orchestrator is the last callable in its task cell. It validates the task's inputs, calls the task's step callables in their contractual order and writes the task's artefacts. The step callables and private helpers are described inside their parent task, not counted separately.

Python implementation author: **CS Chirinda**.

**Sources.** Each description is taken from the orchestrator's signature and documented contract, from the docstrings and code of the step callables it calls, and from the notebook's `STUDY_CONFIG`. The methodological standard is the paper, Cipollina and De Benedictis (2026), *From Tariff Changes to Trade Effects: A Practical Framework for Empirical Trade Policy Analysis* ( i.e. the `latex context`). Quotations come from that source.

**How the 27 tasks fit together.** The pipeline follows the paper's four-step sequence, tariff shock → market access → elasticity → trade effect, plus the two-mode literature map of Section 1.1:

- Tasks 1–3: input validation, cleansing, the test battery and the disclosure ledger.
- Tasks 4–6 (Chain A): the literature space, coding, Gower distances and layout.
- Tasks 7–11 (Chain B, measurement): panel, competitive node set, CES weights, benchmark, margin.
- Tasks 12–15 (elasticity): identification, PPML, the $\beta\to\sigma$ map, the fixed point.
- Tasks 16–19 (counterfactual): scenario, counterfactual margins, trade effects, attribution.
- Tasks 20–24 (reporting and audit): Tables 1–3, the algebraic tests, sign patterns, the calibration hazard.
- Task 25: the end-to-end run.
- Task 26: the sensitivity grid.
- Task 27: the deposit.

**Format of each entry.** Each task has the same seven fields: Inputs, Process, Outputs, Transformation (how input data become output data), Research role (the element of the paper that the callable implements, with a quotation), Equations, and Implementation distinction (where the code makes a declared choice the paper leaves open).

**Notation.**

- Indices: product (HS6 market) $k$; supplier $i$ in the competitive node set $V_{kt}$, which includes the domestic node $\mathrm{DOM}$; year $t$; state $s\in\lbrace0,CF\rbrace$; ISIC Rev. 3 industry $g$.
- Tariffs and weights: applied tariff $\tau_{ikt}$; CES weight $\alpha_i$; elasticity of substitution $\sigma_k$; reference tariff $T_{kt}$.
- Derived quantities: bilateral tariff margin $btm_{ikt}$; benchmark weight $\omega_{ik}$; trade coefficient $\beta_k$; imports $M_{ikt}$; baseline trade $M^0_{ik}$.

Equation numbers are the paper's:

$$
(3)\ \ 1+T_{kt}=\left[\sum_{i}\alpha_i(1+\tau_{ikt})^{1-\sigma_k}\right]^{\frac{1}{1-\sigma_k}},\qquad (4)\ \ btm_{ikt}=\frac{1+T_{kt}}{1+\tau_{ikt}},\qquad (14)\ \ E[M_{ikt}\mid X]=\exp\left[\beta_k\ln btm_{ikt}+\gamma_{it}+\delta_{kt}\right],
$$

$$
(16)\ \ \Delta Trade_{ik}=\frac{\widehat M_{ik}(btm^1_{ik})-\widehat M_{ik}(btm^0_{ik})}{\widehat M_{ik}(btm^0_{ik})},\qquad (17)\ \ TradeEffect_{ik}=M^0_{ik}\thinspace\Delta Trade_{ik},\qquad (19)\ \ \Delta\ln BTM_{ikt}=-\Delta\ln(1+\tau_{ikt})+\Delta\ln(1+T_{kt}).
$$

**Data boundary.** Every published figure below (Table 1 elasticities and margins, Table 2 and Table 3 effects, the Gower distances 0.11, 0.28 and 0.33) is a comparison target that the code checks, never a value an orchestrator returns as computed. The notebook's end-to-end execution (Task 25) runs on a frozen synthetic fixture of 10 markets with 6 nodes each (five foreign suppliers plus the domestic node), so its arithmetic is verified against known answers rather than against the paper's data. Every choice the paper leaves open is tagged `[G]`, `[X]` or `[UNSPECIFIED IN MANUSCRIPT]` in `STUDY_CONFIG` and registered in the disclosure ledger of Task 3.

- **Transformation**: each frame passes three schema layers (exact column set, admissible dtype, nullability) and seven domain predicates, and is fingerprinted. Each configuration leaf is tested for its provenance tag (`S`, `I`, `G` or `X`) before its value range; a `G` leaf must cite a registered imputation id. Step 3 checks the chain interface, fixed-point degeneracy, denominator consistency and the conservation-versus-paper-target relation.
- **Research role**: freezes and fingerprints the inputs of Section 4: "bilateral and domestic trade flows from the US International Trade and Production Database for Estimation (ITPDE) with applied and MFN tariffs from WITS–TRAINS".

\sigma_k>1,\qquad \sum_i\alpha_i=1,\qquad \alpha_i\ge0,\qquad \Delta^{\mathrm{inc}}\in(0,\thinspace0.5],\qquad \sigma^{\mathrm{excl}}=2 .
$$

$$
\tau^{\mathrm{AVE}}_{ikt}\leftarrow\min\left(\tau^{\mathrm{AVE}}_{ikt},\ \bar\tau\right),\qquad w_{h\to g}=\frac{M_{h\to g}}{\sum_{g'}M_{h\to g'}},\qquad 15=7+4+4\ \mathrm{ledger\ rows}.

r_j=\lvert\mathrm{lhs}_j-\mathrm{rhs}_j\rvert\le\varepsilon_j;\quad \varepsilon_{T1-T3}=\mathrm{exact},\ \varepsilon_{T4,T10,T11,T13}=10^{-12},\ \varepsilon_{T5,T6}=10^{-6},\ \varepsilon_{T7,T8,T15}=10^{-15},\ \varepsilon_{T9}=10^{-14},\ \varepsilon_{T12,T14}=10^{-10},\ \varepsilon_{T16}=10^{-2}.

### Task 4 — `run_task_4`

- **Inputs**:
  - keyword `study_corpus` or `task1_result`, `studies_manifest`, `study_config`, `element_codebook`, `excluded_elements`;
  - `primary_assignments` (coder C1), and either `second_assignments` or `llm_response_texts`, with `second_coder_id` (`C2` or `LLM_A`);
  - `adjudication_decisions`, `output_dir`, `bootstrap_replicates` (1,000), `kappa_seed`.
- **Process**: assembles the primary pass and the independent second pass, computes per-element Cohen's $\kappa$ with a percentile bootstrap interval ($\alpha=0.05$), then adjudicates disagreements against the 0.80 threshold.
- **Outputs**: `df_affiliation_matrix`, `adjudication_record`, `kappa` (with `ci_lower`, `ci_upper`, `n_bootstrap`), `below_threshold`, `degenerate`, `task1_result_received`, `paths`, `llm_disclosure`.
- **Transformation**: study abstracts become 17 × 9 codes, each non-zero code backed by a verbatim, offset-exact justification. A non-consensus cell takes the adjudicated value and the written definition invoked.
- **Research role**: implements Section 1.1, step two: "The coding is based on the abstract alone", and "An element takes the value 1 when the abstract claims it, 0.5 when the claim is partial or implicit, and 0 otherwise".
- **Equations**: $\kappa$ treats $0$, $0.5$, $1$ and `FLAG` as four nominal categories:

$$
c_{se}\in\lbrace0,\thinspace0.5,\thinspace1\rbrace,\qquad \kappa_e=\frac{p^{o}_e-p^{c}_e}{1-p^{c}_e},\qquad p^{c}_e=\sum_{q}p^{(1)}_{eq}\thinspace p^{(2)}_{eq},\qquad \kappa_e\ge0.80 .
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the `FLAG` refuse-on-ambiguity category is admitted only in the second pass. A degenerate element ($p^c_e=1$) reports $\kappa$ as NaN with a note. The `LLM_A` branch carries a disclosure, because automated coding is an addition to, not a reproduction of, the published method.

### Task 5 — `run_task_5`

- **Inputs**: `df_affiliation_matrix`, `df_study_corpus`, `output_dir`, `upstream_task_1_output` (echoed by identity, never read).
- **Process**: resolves the three anchor studies by author-surname stems (the reference paper, the merged Cipollina–Salvatici entry, Fugazza and Nicita), computes the Gower matrix, verifies the grid, and scores the three published targets.
- **Outputs**: `task`, `stages`, `distance_matrix` (17 × 17), `half_unit_matrix` ($18D$, integer), `target_report`, `n_pairs` (136), `n_distinct_values`, `verdict`, `artefacts`, `upstream_task_1_output`.
- **Transformation**: the 17 × 9 coding matrix becomes pairwise mean absolute differences, then exact integers on the half-unit grid.
- **Research role**: implements Section 1.1, step three. The merged Cipollina et al. (2017) and Cipollina and Salvatici (2020) entry lies at 0.11 from the reference paper, "Fugazza and Nicita (2013) at 0.28, while every other study in the sample lies at 0.33 or beyond".
- **Equations**: codes on $\lbrace0,0.5,1\rbrace$ put $D$ on the grid of eighteenths, so the targets are 2, 5 and at least 6 half-units:

$$
D_{ss'}=\frac19\sum_{e=1}^{9}\lvert c_{se}-c_{s'e}\rvert,\qquad 18D_{ss'}\in\lbrace0,1,\dots,18\rbrace,\qquad 0.11\approx\tfrac{2}{18},\ \ 0.28\approx\tfrac{5}{18},\ \ 0.33\approx\tfrac{6}{18}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: T1 (zero diagonal), T2 (symmetry) and T3 (integrality) are checked before acceptance. Computed values are never rounded toward a target and no code is altered to meet one; a miss is classified for remediation.

### Task 6 — `run_task_6`

- **Inputs**: `df_affiliation_matrix`, `df_study_corpus`, `upstream_task_5_output` or an explicit `half_unit_matrix`, `upstream_task_2_output` (unused), `output_dir`.
- **Process**: builds the nine element vectors and the claim matrix, places each study at its barycentre (zero-claim studies at the origin), repels overlapping nodes while testing order preservation, audits the layout and writes five artefacts.
- **Outputs**: `output_dir`, `coordinates`, `coordinates_original`, `displacements`, `tau_pre`, `tau_post`, `rho_post`, `n_pairs`, `sweeps_run`, `diagnostics`.
- **Transformation**: codes become planar coordinates. Repulsion moves the two nodes of a close pair symmetrically along their joining line, so the pair's midpoint is preserved; Kendall $\tau$ and Spearman $\rho$ compare the $D$ ordering with Euclidean separations.
- **Research role**: implements Section 1.1, step four: "each study is drawn at the barycentre of the elements it claims, weighted by the strength of the claim", and "a small repulsion term separates overlapping nodes without altering that ordering".
- **Equations**: for a pair closer than the threshold $d^\ast$, with $d_{ij}=\lVert p_i-p_j\rVert$:

$$
u_e=R(\cos\theta_e,\sin\theta_e),\qquad p_s=\frac{\sum_e c_{se}u_e}{\sum_e c_{se}},\qquad \delta=\min(\delta_{\max},\thinspace d^\ast-d_{ij}),\quad p_i\leftarrow p_i+\tfrac{\delta}{2}\hat u_{ij},\quad p_j\leftarrow p_j-\tfrac{\delta}{2}\hat u_{ij}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: sweeps stop once $\tau\ge0.95$ and moves are negligible. A final $\tau$ below 0.95 is reported as a finding, not forced to 1. The nine-row diagnostic records the figure's published state:
  - 18 edges drawn in the source, against 153 possible study–element pairs;
  - 5 solid reference edges in the text, against 3 in the figure;
  - degree 0 for element E7;
  - three unused legend categories.

### Task 7 — `run_task_7`

- **Inputs**: `task_2_output` (`step1`, `step2`, `step3`), `study_config`; keyword `allocation_rule` (`"trade_value_proportional"`), `concordance_version`, `task_1_output`, `output_dir`.
- **Process**: harmonises HS flows onto ISIC Rev. 3 destinations, assembles the long-form analysis panel with tariffs, then partitions it into the five exclusion buckets.
- **Outputs**: `flows`, `allocation_report`, `panel`, `panel_report`, `exclusion_table`, `unmapped_count`, `unmapped_trade_share`, `total_source_value`, `total_allocated_value`, `upstream`.
- **Transformation**: each flow is split across its ISIC destinations, conserving value within $10^{-6}$ relative; unmatched flows keep their value and are flagged. The baseline tariff is resolved by an ordered cascade.
- **Research role**: implements Section 3, step one: "the policy experiment should be defined at the most disaggregated level at which tariff treatment differs across exporters and products".
- **Equations**:

$$
M_{igt}=\sum_h w_{h\to g}M_{iht},\quad \sum_g w_{h\to g}=1;\qquad \tau^0_{ikt}=\begin{cases}0,& i=\mathrm{DOM}\cr \tau^{\mathrm{APPLIED}}_{ikt},&\mathrm{if\ observed}\cr \min\left(\tau^{\mathrm{PREF}}_{ikt},\tau^{\mathrm{MFN}}_{ikt}\right),&\mathrm{otherwise\ if\ either\ exists}\cr \mathrm{missing},&\mathrm{otherwise.}\end{cases}
$$

- **Implementation distinction**:
  - The applied rate is never replaced by the preferential rate, because it already embeds utilisation.
  - Exclusion precedence: missing tariff > missing concordance > missing flow > $\sigma_k=2$ filter.
  - $\sigma_k=2$ cells are flagged, not removed, and the panel itself is never mutated.

### Task 8 — `run_task_8`

- **Inputs**: `task_7_output` (its `panel`), `study_config` (`node_augmentation`); keyword `pre_filter_absorption_usd`, `task_1_output`, `output_dir`.
- **Process**: bridges the Task 7 schema, builds the foreign node set of each market, adds the domestic node under rule `G_6b`, and tabulates the identification-critical properties.
- **Outputs**: exactly `foreign`, `augmented` (`augmented_nodes`, `domestic_presence_status`) and `identification`.
- **Transformation**: per market, membership is gated on `flow_status == "positive"`: missing is not zero, and aggregate codes never enter. Per cell it records $\lvert V_k\rvert$, coverage, the domestic share and the median MFN rate; per industry, the Spearman correlation of $\lvert V_k\rvert$ with the MFN rate (descriptive only).
- **Research role**: implements the benchmark population of Section 4: "Domestic US trade is included because US producers form part of the competitive benchmark faced by foreign suppliers, although domestic transactions are not subject to import tariffs."
- **Equations**:

$$
V_{kt}=V^{\mathrm{foreign}}_{kt}\cup\lbrace\mathrm{DOM}\rbrace\ \ \mathrm{iff\ DOM\ is\ present},\qquad \tau^{s}_{\mathrm{DOM},kt}=0\ \ \forall s\in\lbrace0,CF\rbrace,\qquad s^{d}_{kt}=\frac{M_{\mathrm{DOM},kt}}{\sum_{i\in V_{kt}}M_{ikt}}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: a zero domestic tariff is a permanent identifying assumption, and any configuration that declares otherwise is rejected. Industries with $s^d\gt0.95$ or $s^d\lt0.30$ are flagged. The coverage denominator is the declared pre-filter absorption and is never recomputed.

### Task 9 — `run_task_9`

- **Inputs**: `ingested` (bilateral, domestic, WITS), `study_config`, `sigma_by_product`; optional `task_1_output`, `isic_by_product`, `design` (`"B"`, time-varying, or `"A"`, fixed at `baseline_year`), `flow_floor` ($10^{-6}$), `output_dir`.
- **Process**: builds the foreign panel and its applied-tariff lookup (ceiling-capped; cells without a usable rate dropped under `drop_supplier_from_V_k`), adds `DOM` at $\tau=0$, then computes shares, inverts for $\alpha$ and verifies T13.
- **Outputs**: `shares`, `alpha`, `t13`, `share_sum_min`, `share_sum_max`, `alpha_design`, `alpha_baseline_year`, `alpha_label`, `t13_residual_trajectory` and its path, `output_dir`, drop and cap counters, `task_1_provenance`.
- **Transformation**: positive flows become market shares; shares and tariff factors become normalised CES weights that reproduce the shares exactly.
- **Research role**: supplies the weights of Eq. (3), where "$\alpha_i$ represents the corresponding normalized CES weight, with $\sum_i \alpha_i = 1$". The paper gives no rule for constructing them.
- **Equations**: the exact inversion of the CES share condition:

$$
s_{ik}=\frac{M_{ik}}{\sum_{j\in V_k}M_{jk}},\qquad \tilde\alpha_i=s_{ik}(1+\tau_{ik})^{\sigma_k-1},\qquad \alpha_i=\frac{\tilde\alpha_i}{\sum_j\tilde\alpha_j},\qquad \max_{i,k}\left\lvert\frac{\alpha_i(1+\tau_{ik})^{1-\sigma_k}}{(1+T_k)^{1-\sigma_k}}-s_{ik}\right\rvert\le10^{-12}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: $\alpha$ is labelled "calibrated, not identified". T13 certifies internal consistency only: a wrong $\sigma_k$ still passes because both sides use it. Under Design A, the weights frozen at the baseline year need not reproduce other years' shares.

### Task 10 — `run_task_10`

- **Inputs**: `markets` (`product_code`, `year`, `alpha`, `tau`, `sigma`, the domestic node included), `study_config` (limit constants $\sigma=1.0001$, $\sigma=1000$, tolerance $10^{-6}$), `output_dir`, `upstream_artefact`.
- **Process**: computes the baseline benchmark in log space, audits the conditioning of each market, verifies the two analytic limits on declared fixtures, and writes the ledger and conditioning report.
- **Outputs**: `benchmark` (`one_plus_t`, `ln_one_plus_t`, shifted-argument extrema), `limits`, `conditioning`, `n_markets`, `upstream_received`, `output_dir`, two CSV paths, `upstream_artefact_keys`, `limit_fixtures`.
- **Transformation**: each market's weights, tariffs and elasticity become one reference factor through a max-shifted log-sum-exp, which avoids underflow at large $\sigma_k$ (Table 1 reaches 64.33).
- **Research role**: implements Eq. (3): "Under CES preferences, the reference tariff for product $k$ can be expressed as" a power mean of tariff factors with exponent $1-\sigma_k$.
- **Equations**:

$$
\ln(1+T_k)=\frac{1}{1-\sigma_k}\ \mathrm{LSE}_{i}\left[\ln\alpha_i+(1-\sigma_k)\ln(1+\tau_{ik})\right],\qquad \lim_{\sigma\to\infty}(1+T_k)=\min_i(1+\tau_i),\qquad \lim_{\sigma\to1}(1+T_k)=\prod_i(1+\tau_i)^{\alpha_i}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the orchestrator raises `RuntimeError`, blocking the pipeline, when the largest limit deviation exceeds the tolerance. Markets with condition number $\max_i v_i/\min_i v_i$ above the threshold are flagged and retained, never dropped.

### Task 11 — `run_task_11`

- **Inputs**: `ingested` (bilateral, domestic, WITS, concordance, elasticities), `study_config` (baseline year 2017, ceiling, HS6 level, aggregate codes), `output_dir`, `upstream_report`.
- **Process**: normalises flows to the baseline year and selects tariffs, then computes shares, weights and benchmarks through the verified Task 9 and Task 10 callables. It then builds the margins and runs T7, T4 and T8.
- **Outputs**: four artefact paths (`task_11_margin_cells.csv`, `task_11_industry_summary.csv`, `task_11_market_omega_sums.csv`, `task_11_attribution.json`), `n_cells`, `n_markets`, `max_recompute_abs_diff`, `max_omega_deviation`, `max_attribution_residual`, `baseline_year`, `upstream_report_present`.
- **Transformation**:
  - Each cell's tariff and its market's benchmark become a margin.
  - T7 recomputes that margin from raw $\alpha$, $\sigma$ and $\tau$.
  - T8 runs on a synthetic state in which every foreign tariff rises by a declared increment and DOM does not.
- **Research role**: implements Eq. (4): "Values above one indicate more favorable tariff treatment than the reference tariff, while values below one indicate a relative disadvantage."
- **Equations**:

$$
btm_{ikt}=\frac{1+T_{kt}}{1+\tau_{ikt}},\qquad \omega_{ik}=\frac{\alpha_i(1+\tau_{ik})^{1-\sigma_k}}{(1+T_k)^{1-\sigma_k}},\qquad \sum_{i\in V_k}\omega_{ik}=1\ \ (\pm10^{-12}).
$$

- **Implementation distinction**:
  - T4 uses the stored benchmark rather than a recomputation, so it is not a tautology.
  - T8 must stay strictly below $10^{-15}$, and a zero increment is rejected as a trivial test.
  - Products without an elasticity or an industry mapping are recorded, not silently dropped.

### Task 12 — `run_task_12`

- **Inputs**: `panel` (`product_code`, `year`, `exporter_iso3`, `flow_value`, `tau`, `T`, and others), `study_config` (shipped `design_choice = "Design_B_over_identified"`), `output_dir`, `upstream`.
- **Process**: runs the Design A rank test, estimates Design B as a diagnostic, overrides the design choice to `Design_A_restricted` on a deep copy of the configuration, builds the four-row DesignReport, and writes the selection JSON and the replication-note head.
- **Outputs**: `design_choice`, `amended_design_choice`, `diagnostic_design`, `design_A_stationarity`, `finding`, `design_b`, `design_report`, `g_design_override`, `g14b`, `upstream_provenance`, `upstream_keys`, `output_files`, `output_dir`.
- **Transformation**: the panel's fixed-effect design is extended with $\ln(1+T_{kt})$ and the rank increase is measured. A zero increase means the benchmark adds no identifying variation.
- **Research role**: tests the paper's Eq. (14) design, in which "exporter-time and product-time fixed effects absorb unobserved determinants of trade" in "the single-importer setting considered here".
- **Equations**: $\ln(1+T_{kt})$ varies only in $(k,t)$, so it is absorbed by $\delta_{kt}$, and only own-tariff variation identifies $\beta_k$:

$$
\beta_k\ln btm_{ikt}+\delta_{kt}=-\beta_k\ln(1+\tau_{ikt})+\underbrace{\delta_{kt}+\beta_k\ln(1+T_{kt})}_{\tilde\delta_{kt}};\qquad \mathrm{B}:\ \ln\mu=\beta^{\mathrm{own}}_k\ln(1+\tau)+\beta^{\mathrm{bm}}_k\ln(1+T)+\gamma_{it}+\delta_k+\delta_t+\theta\ln E_{kt},\quad H_0:\beta^{\mathrm{bm}}_k=-\beta^{\mathrm{own}}_k .
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the caller's configuration is never mutated. The override is recorded as ledger entry `G_design_override`, citing the identity argument. The expenditure proxy $E_{kt}=\sum_iM_{ikt}$ is declared `[X]` as `G_14b`.

### Task 13 — `run_task_13`

- **Inputs**: `panel` (zero flows retained), `study_config` (`estimation_settings`), `output_dir`, `design` (`"Design_A_restricted"` by default), `sigma_map` (`"sigma = beta + 1"`), `task1_output` (only `verdict` read).
- **Process**: absorbs the high-dimensional fixed effects by alternating projections. It then estimates PPML by IRLS, applying the separation rule once (detect, drop, refit), and attaches cluster-robust inference and the $\sigma$ delta method to every estimate.
- **Outputs**: a JSON-ready report, including:
  - the echoed estimation settings;
  - absorption convergence and iterations, `fe_group_names`, `separation_count`, `separation_trade_share`, `converged`;
  - `zero_flows_retained`, `estimates`, `n_estimates`, `upstream_task1_verdict`, `report_path`.
- **Transformation**: under Design A, each industry's cells become one $\hat\beta_k$ on $\ln btm$, with exporter-time and product-time effects. Each estimate carries CGM (primary) and CR1 (sensitivity) standard errors, with two-way clustering on exporter and product-time.
- **Research role**: implements Section 3, step three: "PPML provides a natural estimator for this class of models because it accommodates zero trade flows and remains consistent in the presence of heteroskedasticity".
- **Equations**: the PPML first-order conditions over observations $n$, and the declared map with its delta-method error:

$$
\sum_{n}\left(M_n-\exp(x_n^\top b)\right)x_n=0,\qquad \hat\sigma_k=\hat\beta_k+1,\qquad \mathrm{se}(\hat\sigma_k)=\left\lvert\frac{\partial\hat\sigma_k}{\partial\hat\beta_k}\right\rvert\mathrm{se}(\hat\beta_k)=\mathrm{se}(\hat\beta_k).
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the report records that the paper publishes no $\hat\beta_k$ (`parameter_estimates_reported_in_paper = 0`). Absorption method, tolerance $10^{-8}$, iteration cap 1,000, separation rule and clustering are all declared imputations.

### Task 14 — `run_task_14`

- **Inputs**: `study_config`, `output_dir`, `design` (`"Design_A_calibration"` by default; `"Design_A_restricted"` or `"Design_B_over_identified"` for re-estimation), `task13_output`, `task1_output`.
- **Process**:
